The Story

Twenty-three years behind the chair. One reason to keep going.

My nan has lived with vascular dementia for eighteen years. She taught me patience, empathy and dignity. A year of hands-on work in care homes showed me what a familiar ritual can do for a gentleman whose world has become confusing.

These men built our towns, raised families and lived lives full of graft and pride. They deserve to leave the chair feeling like the man they were decades ago, walking out of the barbershop.

The Experience

Every item in the kit sparks a memory

  1. I.A screen built into a vintage television case playing familiar adverts, game show jingles and music videos from his era.
  2. II.A vintage radio humming warmly in the background, never clinical silence.
  3. III.A personalised digital photo frame with a folder for each resident, filled with photos their families provide: wedding days, army recruitment, childhood, holidays.
  4. IV.The full hot towel setup, the ritual almost no mobile barber brings into a care home.
