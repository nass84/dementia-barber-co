The value

What you actually get

Beyond a tidy haircut, this is a wellbeing session that fits into your routines instead of interrupting them.

Residents settle

A predictable visit, a familiar face and a routine they can rely on. Gentlemen who have been unsettled all week often open up the moment the clippers switch on.

Pressure comes off your team

Grooming handled properly, safely and without drama. No anxious resident to talk down afterwards, and no room left for staff to put back together.

Families are reassured

Relatives see their husband, dad or grandad looking like himself. It is one of the most visible signs that a home is looking after the person, not just the condition.

Meaningful activity, provided

The vintage television, the radio and the personalised photographs turn a haircut into a memory session — the kind of one-to-one engagement your team rarely has time to facilitate.

A service that stays

I turn up when I say I will. Homes I worked with before starting out on my own asked to stay with me rather than lose the consistency we’d built.

Nothing left for you to do

I sign in, set up, clean down between residents and hand the space back exactly as I found it. Waste goes into your clinical or general stream, as your policy dictates.

Compliance

Everything your team will ask for

Happy to send documentation across before a first visit, or to bring hard copies with me.

  • Public liability insurance — certificate available on request
  • Enhanced DBS check — current, and available to view
  • Risk assessments and method statements for grooming in a care setting
  • Safeguarding policy — available on request
  • References from care home managers — contactable on request

Infection control

Tools cleaned, disinfected with professional-grade solution and stored correctly before I arrive, with clipper blades cleaned between every resident. A fresh gown and a disposable neck strip for each gentleman, and towels are never reused. Hands washed and sanitised before and after every resident. PPE worn to your policy. A UV hot towel cabinet for disinfection on site. Hair, tissue and disposable PPE bagged and disposed of through your stream. Kit wiped down at the end of the session and fully sanitised again at home.

Booking

How it works in practice

  1. Get in touch

    Tell me roughly how many gentlemen you have and where you are. I’ll come back with rates and the next available dates.

  2. A first visit, no commitment

    Half day or full day. Plenty of homes start with a single visit to see how residents respond before putting anything in the diary long term.

  3. A standing monthly slot

    This works best every four to five weeks, or around the same date each month. Regular visits build familiarity far more quickly than occasional ones.

  4. Your activities coordinator takes the reins

    Once we’re up and running they usually become my main contact — organising the day and keeping residents flowing. We normally book the next session, or block-book the next few months, before I leave.

Current availability

Visits run on Tuesdays and Wednesdays, roughly 10am to 3pm depending on numbers, within about 50 miles of Birmingham. That is growing through 2026 — if those days don’t suit, ask anyway and I’ll see what I can do.
